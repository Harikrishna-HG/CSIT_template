Farwestern University - Central Department Of CSIT

The B.sc CSIT programme was started in 2069 B.S as first batch of such kind with only 6 students. The department has been growing in the terms of space, hardware, software and manpower. Science and Technology faculty has been struggling for its existence since the establishment of Far Western University. However dedicated teacher, staff and students collectively working for the stability and prosperity of faculty. The CSIT programme was established as separate CSITdepartment in end of 2074 BS.

The bachelors of Science in computer Science and Information Technology(B.Sc. CSIT) is a 4 year semester course of Far Western University designed to provide the students with all sorts of knowledge in the field of computer science and information technology.

Message From Vice-Chancellor

Far Western University (FWU) is recently established governmental institution with a clear vision for its role in shaping the future. By focusing on quality education and research, it's positioned to equip students with the cutting-edge skills and knowledge needed to tackle the challenges of the 21st century. And the emphasis on nurturing leaders suggests a commitment to developing not just academics, but individuals who can drive positive change in their communities and beyond. In the face of different circumstances, we find inspiration and optimism in the steadfast expressions of the FWU family. Talented young lions, the core value of FWU, should be granted a space to unleash their boundless powers and imagination. As you all aspire to see FWU as a center of excellence in higher education, I assure you that we can maintain the equilibrium among every component associated with the institute. I believe on “think globally without losing sight of our national and even up to local area” to provide proper direction for the enhancement of higher education. Our teamwork focuses on the sociocultural value of our communities and explores novel research areas of the 21st century. This entails not only advancing knowledge and learning but also fostering the creation of new knowledge. Excited about the journey ahead, I eagerly anticipated collaborating with all of you to elevate this emerging university to a new height.
